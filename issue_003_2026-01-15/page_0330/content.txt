Subject: Lebanon Fiscal Management Project
Notification of Board Approval

Excellency,

We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Executive Directors of the International
Bank for Reconstruction and Development and the International Development Association (the
Bank) has approved on February 14, 2024, a loan in the amourt of twenty eight million and
five hundred thousand United States Dollars (USS28,500,000), in addition to a grant from the
Lebaron Financing Facility (LFF) for five million and five hundred thousand United States
Dollars (US$5,500,000) for the Lebanon Fiscal Management Project. The development
objective of the Fiscal Management Project is to restore the basic functions for domestic
revenue mobilization and accountable allocation and use of public resources.

We look forward to an early signing of the Loan Agreement and Grant Agreement, and
swift ratification of the loan by the Lebanese Parliament.

Sincerely yours,
