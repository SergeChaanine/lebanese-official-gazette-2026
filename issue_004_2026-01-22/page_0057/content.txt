‎On the basis of the verbal note No. WZ444.00 of the Government of the Federal Republic
of Germany dated 30 November 2018, and the confirmation note No. 1047/15 of the
Government of the Republic of Lebanon dated 07 December 2020, the Recipient, the
Project-Executing Agency and KiW hereby enter into the following grant and project
Agreement (the "Agreement":

‎Amount and Purpase of the Grant

‎KfW shall extend to the Recipient a grant not exceeding

‎EUR 15.000,000.00 (in words: fifteen million euros)

‎(the "Grant”).

‎The Grant shall not be repayable unless otherwise stipulated in Article 4.2.

‎The Recipient shall channel the Grant in full to the Project-Executing Agency in
accordance with the conditions set forth in Article 2. The Project-Executing Agency
shall use the Grant exclusively for financing the rehabiitation and extension of
water supply and waste water systems in host communities for Syrian refugees as
well as consulting services (the "Project”) and primarily to finance the foreign
exchange costs. The Project-Executing Agency and KiW shall determine the
details of the Project and the goods anc services to be financed from the Grant by
a separate agreement (the "Separate Agreement’).

‎Taxes and other public charges io be bome by the Recipient or the Project-
Executing Agency and import duties shall not be financed from the Grant.

‎Channelling of the Grant to the Project-Executing Agency

‎The Recipient shall channel the Grant to the Project-Executing Agency as a non-
repayable grant under a separate financing agreement.

‎Prior to the first disbursement from the Grant, the Recipient shall furnish to KfW a
copy cr a certified translation of the agreement referred to in Article 2.1.

‎The channelling of the Grant shall not constitute any liability of the Project-
Executing Agency to KfW for payment oblications uncer this Agreement.
