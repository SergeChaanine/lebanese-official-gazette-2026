3, Disbursement

3.1 KW shall disburse the Grant in accordance with the progress of the Project and
upon request of the Project-Executing Agency. By the Separate Agreement, the
Project-Executing Agency and KfW shall determine the disbursement procedure,
in particular the evidence proving that the requested funds are used for the

stipulated purpose.

3.2 KfW shall have the right 10 refuse to make disbursements after 15 December 2027.

4. Suspension of Disbursement and Repayment
4.1 KfW may not suspend disbursements unless

a) the Recipient fails to perform its obligations to KfW to make payments when
due,

b} obligations under this Agreement or under separate agreements pertinent to
this Agreement have been violated,

c) the Project-Executng Agency is unable to prove that the disbursed amounts
have all been used for the stipulated purpose,

d) the fulfilment of KfWWs obligations under this Agreement violates applicable
law, or

e) extraordinary circumstances arise that preclude or senously jeopardize the
implementation, the operation, or the purpose of the Project.

least 30 days, KIW may,

a) in the case of Article 4.1 b) or d), demand the immediate repayment of all

b) in the case of Article 4.1 c), demand the immediate repayment of such

used for the stipulated purpose.
