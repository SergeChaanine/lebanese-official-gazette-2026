4. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement
shall first be resolved through
friendly consultations between the
Parties.

If the dispute cannot be resolved
through consultation within a
reasonable period of time, the
Parties agree to refer the dispute to
Lebanese Courts mutually agreed
upon by the Parties. The arbitral
award shall be final and binding

upon both Parties.
