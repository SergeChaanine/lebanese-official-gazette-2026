Article 9 - Amendment

9.1 Any amendment to this Financing Agreement shall be made in writing, including through an
exchange of letters.

Article 10 - Suspension

10.1 The Commission may suspend the Financing Agreement without prior notice in the following
cases:

a) the Partner breaches any obligation set under this Financing Agreement;

b) the Partner fails to respect the principles of international law, including the principles as
referred to in the United Nations Charter. the principles of democracy, human dignity,
equality. the rule of law or good governance, or respect for human rights, including the rights
of persons belongings to minorities, and freedoms or for internationally recogmised nuclear
safety standards or in serious cases of corruption;

c) force majeure. "Force majeure” shall mean any unforeseeable and exceptional situation or
event beyond the Parties’ control which prevents either of them from fulfilling any of their
obligations, not attributable to emor or negligence on their part (or the part of their
contractors, agents or employees) and proves insurmountable in spite of all due diligence.
Neither of the Parties shall be held liable for breach of its obligations under this Financing
Agreement if it is prevented from fulfilling them by force majeure, provided it takes
measures to minimise any possible damage.

d) In cases such as crisis or change of position at national level, including its policy priorities.

10.2 The Commission may take any appropriate precautionary measure before suspending.

10.3 The consequences of such suspension on the Action shall be aaalysed and determined on a case-
by-case basis, including the impact on on-going implementing contracts and agreements.

10.4 A suspension of this Financing Agreement is without prejudice to the termination of this

Financing Agreement in accordance with Article 11.

10.5 The Parties shall resume the implementation of the Financing Agreement once the conditions
allow. This is without prejudice to any amendments of this Financing Agreement, which may bé

necessary to adapt the Action to the new impiementing conditions, including, if possible, the
extension of the execution period, or the termination of this Financing Agreement in accordance

with Articie 11.

Article 11 - Termination

11.1 If the issues which led to the suspension of this Financing Agreement have not been resolved
within 2 maximum period of 180 days, either party may terminate this Financing Agreement 1
30 days’ notice.

11.2 Without prejudice to Article 11.1 above, if at any time, the Commission believes that the purpose
of this Financing Agreement can no longer effectively or appropriately performed, this Financing
Agreement may be terminated by serving 30 days written motivated notice.

11.3 The consequences of such terminations on the ongoing activities may be anaiysed, where relevant.
and determined on a case-by-case basis.
