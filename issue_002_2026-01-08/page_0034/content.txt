The European Commission, hereinafter referred to as the "Commission", acting on behalf of the

have agreed as follows:

Article 1 - Financing of the Action

1.1 The Partner agrees that the EU finances the implementation of the following action described in
Annex I : ‘EU support to renewable energy for security forces’ ACT-62749 (the “Action™).
Annex I summarises the objectives, expected results, implementation modalities and indicative
duration of this action.

1,2 [The total estimated cost of this Action is EUR 8 000 000.00 and the maximum EU contribution
to this Action is set at EUR EUR 8 000 000.00, ‘

Article 2 -Signature and Execution period

2.1 The execution period of this Financing Agreement shall commence on the date of the entry into
force of this agreement and cover the implementation and closure of all the activities described in

Annex I.

The effective end date of the execution period of this Financing Agreement shall be
communicated by the Commission, in writing, to the Partner.

2.3 In case any of the Parties signs the Financing Agreement, amendments to the latter or any other
document required for the implementation of the Financing Agreement using qualified electronic
signature(s), the Parties shall accept the validity of such qualified electronic signature and
recognise the latter as equivalent ro a hand-written signature, provided that it is compliant with
Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 of the Furopean Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on
electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and
repealing Directive 1999/93/EC.
