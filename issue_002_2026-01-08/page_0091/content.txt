Article 14 ~ Entry into force

This Financing Agreement shall enter into force on the date on which the Commission receives a
notification from the Partner confirming the completion of the internal procedures of the Partner
necessary for its entry inte force. The Commission shall inform the Partner of the date of the
receipt of this notification. This Financing Agreement shall not enter into force if such a
notification is not received by the Commission by 31 December 2025.
