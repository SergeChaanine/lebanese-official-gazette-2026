Lebanon has been grappling with an extensive financial crisis since late 2019, exacerbated by the 6010-9

Wile legal frameworks such as the National Anti-Corruption Strategy have been approved and dedicated

The Action is therefore proposed. to tackle corruption and improve govemance in Lebanon through the

development and stability in Lebanon. Accordingly, this Action aims to improve the accessibility and quality
