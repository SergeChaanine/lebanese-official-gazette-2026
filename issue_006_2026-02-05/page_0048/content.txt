ARTICLE 21
Supervisory authority

1 The Parties shall provide for one or more independent public authorities responsible for data
protection to oversee the implementation of, and ensure compliance with this Agreement, for the
purpose of protecting the fundamental rights and freedoms of natural persons in relation to the

processing of personal data.
