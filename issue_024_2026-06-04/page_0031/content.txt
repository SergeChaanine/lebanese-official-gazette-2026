0 ubsidia ent

0) To facilitate the carrying out of the Project, the Recipient, through MoEW,
shall make the proceeds of the grant, available to EDL under a subsidiary
agreement between the Recipient, through MoEW, and EDL (“Subsidiary
Agreement”), under terms and conditions satisfactory to the Bank, which shall
include, inter alia, the following:

(A) the obligation of the EDL to carry out the Project with due diligence and
efficiency, in conformity with appropriate administrative, economic,
engineering, managerial, financial, environmental, social and technical
standards and practices, including but not limited to Anti-Corruption
Guidelines, Procurement Regulations, Environmental and Social
Standards, and provide promptly as needed, the facilities, services and
other resources required for the implementation of the Project;

(B) the obligation of the EDL to maintain, throughout Project implementation,
adequate staffing and personnel at the EDL-PMT and procedures for the
implementation of the Project; all with-terms of reference, qualifications
and experience satisfactory to the Bank, and as further detailed in the
Project Operations Manual;

(C) the obligation of the EDL to: (i) exchange views with the MoEW, and the
Bank with regard to the progress of the Project, and the performance of its
obligations under the Subsidiary Agreement; and (ii) assist the Recipient
in complying with its obligations referred to in Section 2.05 ofthe Annex
to this Agreement;

(D) the obligation of EDL to promptly inform the Recipient, through MoEW,
and the Bank of any condition which interferes or threatens to interfere
with the progress of the Project;
