Article II
Project Execution

2.01. Project Objectives and Description. The objective of the Project is to enable cleaner grid
clectricity services in Lebanon. The Project consists of the following parts:

Part 1: Feasibility studies for solar PY generation

Supporting preliminary studies and feasibility assessments for a solar photovoltaic (PV) generation
facility with battery energy storage system by:

(a) conducting detailed geospatial analysis of the site based on earlier studies and criteria
agreed with Electricité du Liban (EDL) to define technical and engineering specifications;

(b) analyzing and selecting system-technologies, including-specifications for PV panels and
inverters;

(c) using high-resolution modeling to estimate energy outputs based on PV module, inverter,
and equipment characteristics; and

(d) addressing site preparation, equipment costs, control systems, meteorological hardware,
electrical networks, and decommissioning
