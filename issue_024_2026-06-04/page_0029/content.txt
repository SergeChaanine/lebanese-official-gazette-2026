Part 2: Site-specific environmental and social studies as per the Environmental and Social
Framework (ESF)

Supporting EDL in developing site-specific environmental and social instruments to identify potential
environmental, social, and health and safety risks of the solar PV plant’s construction and operation

by:

(a) preparing a site-specific environmental and social impact assessment (ESIA), focusing on
key risks and necessary mitigation measures;

(b) carrying out consultations with stakeholders and integrating their feedback into the
designs and the ESIA; and

(c) preparing terms of reference for the engineering, procurement, and construction
contractor to develop: (1) a construction environmental and social management plan; and
(ii) an occupational health and safety risk assessment. -

2.02. Project Execution Generally. The Recipient declares its commitment to the objectives of
the Project. To this end, the Recipient shall, through the Ministry of Energy and Water (MoEW),
cause EDL to carry out the Project in accordance with the provisions of: (a) Article II of the
Standard Conditions; (b) the Anti-Corruption Guidelines; (c) this Article II; and (d) the Subsidiary
Agreement.

2.03. Institutional and Other Arrangements. Without limitation to the provision of Section
2.02 above, the Recipient, through MoEW, shall:

(a) Project Steering Committee

maintain throughout Project implementation, a steering committee, chaired by the Minister
of Energy and Water, with terms of reference, composition, roles and responsibilities
acceptable to the Bank and defined in the Project Operations Manual (“Project Steering
Committee”), to provide, inter alia: (i) overall strategic guidance and Project oversight;
(ii) approve the Annual Work Plan and Budget; and (iii) review implementation and
evaluation reports, as applicable.
