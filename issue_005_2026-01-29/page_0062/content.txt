Article 15

Terms of Employment

. The employer shall cover the medical insurance, allowances and compensation for work-

related injury including disability of the Ethiopian worker in accordance with the laws and
regulations of the Republic of Lebanon.

. The employer shall not transfer the worker to work in a place other than that specified in the

Employment Contract or to other households.

. The employer shall provide the worker, including domestic worker, with an insurance policy

in accordance with applicable laws, covering healthcare, work emergencies, disability and
death,

. The insurance policy shall be effective for the period of the Employment Contract and shail

be renewed with the renewal of the Employment Contract.

Article 16

Payment and Remittance

. Wages shall be paid based on the terms and conditions provided under this Article and

the employment contract,
Ethiopian worker shall have the right to:
a. Have a bank account in his/her own name as of the first month of employment; and/or

b. Remit his/her income to Ethiopia or elsewhere, at his'her discretion.

. The wages shall be paid monthly from the first month cf histher employment and not after

five days from the date the salary becomes due.

. At the request of the worker, the payment may be paid:

a. Through a bank account opened in the name of the Bopha worker

b. Through a bank account in the worker’s name opened in Ethiopia through the
Embassy or Consulate cf the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia;

c. By transfer to a recipient chosen by the worker through one of the officially

recognized money transfer service providers in the Republic of Lebanon.
