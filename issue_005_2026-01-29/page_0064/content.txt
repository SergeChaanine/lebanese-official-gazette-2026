Article 20
Responsibilities of the Government of the Republic of Lebanon

The Government of the Republic of Lebanon shall:

1. Ensure that applications for the employment of an Ethiopian workers shall indicate the job
specifications, required qualifications, types of jobs for which recruitment is proposed as well
as the terms and conditions of employment offered including wages, non-wage benefits,
accommodation and transportation, allowances provided for in the applicable laws and
regulations and any other details required by the Ministry of Labor of the Republic of
Lebanon; |

2. Ensure that the welfare and rights of workers, including Domestic workers, employed in the

Republic of Lebanon are promoted and that Ethiopian workers enjoy no lesser rights,
privileges, protections than foreign workers engaged in the same employment activities in the
Republic of Lebanon;

3. Give recognition and legal force for the Employment Contract made in accordance with the
model contract of employment;

4. Uphold the rights of Ethiopian workers in the Republic of Lebanon;

5. Ensure that Ethiopian workers, including Domestic workers are not subjected to conditions
of forced labor as defined under the relevant ILO Convention, Declarations and principles;

6. Ensure that Ethiopian workers can remit their incomes to Ethiopia or eisewhere, at their
discretion; and

7. Facilitate the expeditious scttiement of disputes between the employer and the worker filed

before appropriate authorities/courts of the Republic of Lebanon.
