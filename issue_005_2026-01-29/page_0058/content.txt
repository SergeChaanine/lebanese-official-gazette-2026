Article 4

Consultation and Mutual Visits

The two Parties agree to undertake consultations and exchange of visits with the view to achieve

the objectives and purposes of this Agreement.

Article 5
Facilitation

ihe Parties agree to facilitate the working conditions that enable Ethiopian nationals to work in
the Republic of Lebanon within the framework of this Agreement and in accordance with the

Article 6

Competent Authorities

The Ministry of Labor of the Republic of Lebanon and the Ministry of Labor and Skills of the
Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia shall be the competent authorities to monitor,

coordinate and oversee the implementation of this Agreement.
