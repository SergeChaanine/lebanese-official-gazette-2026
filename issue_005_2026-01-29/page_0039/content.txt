Section §
Financial Provisions

Each Participant will cover its own expenses relating to the implementation of this
MOU, unless the Participants mutually decide otherwise in subsequent arrangements.

Section ٠
Notices

The Participants entrust the implementation of this MOU by the respective divisions
within the Ministries. The Points of Contact for the implementation of this MOU are

as follows:

The Minister of National Defence of Lebanon:
Director of International Military Cooperation/Army Staff For Planning

For the Minister of Defence of the Kingdom of The Netherlands:
Director of International Military Cooperation of the Defence Staff

Section ١
Settlement Of Disputes
Any dispute regarding the interpretation or implementation of this MOU is settled only
by consultation between the Participants and is not to be referred to any national or
international tribunal or court or any other third party for settlement.
