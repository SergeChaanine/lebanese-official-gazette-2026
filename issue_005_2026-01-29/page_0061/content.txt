Article 11
Age of Recruitment

The Parties shall monitor the recruitment of workers that are only at age of eighteen (18) years

and above,

Article 12
Training

The Miristry of Labor and Skilis of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia shall facilitate
the provision of training programs for the workers prior to their departure to work in Lebanon

and shall provide them with certificates to this effect,

Article 13
Working Hours and Leave

i. The working hours of the worker shall not exceed the time limit specified in the relevant
legislation in the host country. |
2. The annual paid leave for Ethiopian workers in the Republic of Lebanon shall be without

discrimination and in accordance with Lebanon laws.

Article 14
Combating Illegal Recruitment and Human Trafficking

|. The Parties agree to work together in order to combat illegal recruitment activities and
human traflicking in their countries. ; |

2. The Parties agree to share and exchange relevant information regarding illegal
recruitment activities and human trafficking in their countries.

3. The Parties shall take the necessary legal actions against the perpetrators of illegal

recruitment and human trafficking residing in their jurisdetion.
