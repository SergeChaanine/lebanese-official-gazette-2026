Article 1
Definitions
For the purposes of this Agreement and unless the context provides otherwise, the following
terms and expressions shall have the meanings stated hereunder:
|. “Employment Contract” means a contract between the employer and an Ethiopian worker
that regulates the working conditions anc rights of the worker and which is made in
accordance with this Agreement, and the model employment contract enclosed to this
Agreement,
“Employer” means a natural or juridical person in the Republic of Lebanon who have
employed an Ethiopian worker, including domestic workers to perform werk in accordance

with the employment contract;

. “Domestic Worker” means an Ethiopian migrant worker who have entersd into an

engaged in domestic work performed in a household; and

4. “Worker” means an Ethiopian person who is employed in the Republic of Lebanon,

including domestic worker, and who is engaged or has been engaged in a remunerated

aclivity in the Republic of Lebanon.

Article 2

Objective

the objective of this Agreement is to strengthen the cooperation on labor matters between the
two countries in the field of labor and to provide a legal framework for the employment of
Ethiopian workers, including as Ethiopian Domestic workers and to protect, enhance and enforce
their rights in the Republic of Lebanon.
