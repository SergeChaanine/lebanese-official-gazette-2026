Article 21

Common Responsibilities of the Parties

. The Parties agree to jointly combat illegal recruitment activities and human trafficking in

their countries,

. The Parties shall ensure compliance with their respective laws, rules, and regulations by

employment agencies and take legal action against employment agencics that contravene the
laws of the countries and involve in any conduct that harms the rights of the worker,

including Domestic workers,

. The Parties shall take the necessary administrative measures to prevent agencies and

individuals from working as recruitment bodies in a way that violates this Agreement and the

laws and regulations of either country.

. The Parties shall cooperate to itemize and identify all costs associated with recruitment and

deployment of workers and continuousiy update the itemized list.

. The Parties shall cooperate to combat and take iegal action against all manifestations of

- forced labor and trafficking in persons and to grant workers full and gerder-sensitive legal

The Parties shall cooperate to align their respective occupational standards to facilitate the

acquisition end mutual recognition of the relevant labor skills.
