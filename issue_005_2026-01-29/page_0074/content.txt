14, Settlement of Disputes

14.1. Any claim or complaint shall be settled amicably between the Employer and the

Worker.

14.2, If the Employer and the Worker fail to settle the case amicably between them, the

case shall be referred to the Ministry of Labor of the Republic of Lebanon.

14.3. The Embassy of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia in the Republic of

Lebanon shall be notified about the filing of a complaint. The representative of the

Embassy or consulate of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia can attend and

participate in the process to resolve complaints.

14.4, The worker shall be notified of her/his right to have consular access.

145. If no amicable settlement is reached, the complaint shall be presented to the

competent judicial authorities of the Republic of Lebanon for settlement.

14,6. The Parties shall ensure access of the workers to legal assistance.

15, Renewal of the Employment Contract

Republic of Lebanon. The renewed employment contract shall be informed to the concerned
