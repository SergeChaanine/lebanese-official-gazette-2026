Reconnaissance License SUMMARY,
TGS present our application for a Reconnaissance

License for the acquisition of a new 3D seismic survey
offshore Lebanon.

Our application is in two parts and includes a plan for
the acquisition of new MultiClient Boradband Seismic,
Gravity and Magnetic data near blocks 8, covering an
area without any 3D seismic data, the plan also includes
integrating this new survey with all existing 3D data
offshore Lebanon, merging into one single consistent
and contiguous dataset allowing more efficient and
enhanced geological/geophysical studies. The
application is further enhanced by the commitment of
Institutional Support, a budget for Marketing support,
for the 3" licensing round as well as for the future.

The application would provide TGS the exclusive rights
for marketing and Multi-Client licensing of all seismic
data in Lebanon and would continue our long-standing
commitment with the Ministry of Energy and Water, and
the Petroleum Administration and provide additional
benefit to the state of Lebanon.

Our full enhanced application would provide a unique,
unified and significantly improved database vital for the
promotion of the offshore Lebanon.

We appreciate the guidance of the Ministry and
Petroleum Administration on the way forward.
