4.2.3 Protection of Human Rights and Enhanced Accountability

Protecting human rights and ensuring accountability are fundamental to enhancing
legitimacy, trust and confidence in the ISF, The BPST will continue to work with the
Inspectorate General and the Human Rights Division to strengthen accountability
mechanisms, transparency and human rights monitoring.

٠ Reducing detention overcrowding: To scope and identify and pilot practical
interventions that address drivers of overcrowding, and on reducing delays in the
pre-trial detention process. (ISF Strategic Plan: Program 3.3.1).

« Women and juvenile detention: Assist in designing a model approach to the
management of women and juvenile detainees, to be piloted in Police of Beirut as
part of the community policing roll-out. (ISF Strategic Plan: Project 3.3.1C).

* Human Rights monitoring: Strengthening the Human Rights Division by providing
assistance to professionalise its role in promoting and monitoring human rights
specifically in relation to public order in alignment with the legal texts that govern
the work cf the Division. This will include the development of guidelines, capacity
building, development of tools and methodologies to ensure monitoring is conducted
professionally. BPST will continue to liaise with the Inspectorate General in order to
identify how to support forthcoming investigations of police misconduct. (ISF

Strategic Plan: Project 3.4.1A).
