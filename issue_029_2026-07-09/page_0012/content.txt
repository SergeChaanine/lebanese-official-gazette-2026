Article 6
Background Intellectual Property

Conducting joint activity in the course of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation shall not
create any obligation for the States of the Parties, and/or their natural persons, and/or their legal
entities to provide and/or transfer the Background Intellectual Property which they own.

The Background Intellectual Property may constitute a contribution either of Party, and/or
Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) to the joint activity in the course of

bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation.

Provision and use of the Background Intellectual Property shall be only effected upon ensuring
by the parties and/or Authorized Entities of its legal protection within the territory of the state
where the use of such Intellectual Property is intended .

Article 7
Jointly developed Results of Intellectual Activity

Based on suggestions by Contractors (Subcontractors), the Authorized Entities shall make by
mutual agreement decisions on patenting of the jointly developed Results of intellectual
Activity or on the protection of the information contained in such jointly developed Results of

Intellectual Activity as Trade Secret (Know-How).

The Parties, and/or Authorized Entities and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) shall take
measures for non- disclosure of the information on the jointly developed Results of Intellectual
Activity until the decision is made regarding the form of their legal protection .
