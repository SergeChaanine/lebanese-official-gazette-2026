4. Support to be provided by the British Embassy

4.1 The British Embassy is committed to support the MoIM to strengthen governance and
oversight of reforms and development assistance as well as the MoIM strategic priority
to address prisen and police detention overcrowding. The ‘support will focus on two
areas:

e MoiM Analysis, Planning and Donor Coordination (APC): Assist the MolM to
strengthen donor coordination through the development of an
MolM Donor Coordination Platform and establishment, training and mentoring of a
Analysis, Planning and Donor Coordination Team who will administer the platform,
develop analytical reports, assist in the development of plans and provide technical
assistance to the Minister in overseeing donor assistance. The support will also
include refurbishment and upgrading of the working premises for the Team.

e National Action Plan to Reduce Detention Overcrowding: Support the Ministry of
Interior and Municipalities and Ministry of Justice in measures to reduce
overcrowding and facilitate stakeholder coordination.

4.2 The British Embassy is further committed to support the DG ISF to achieve its strategic
objectives in alignment with the ISF Strategic Plan. Areas for further cooperation are
specified below. References to ISF projects and programmes are Indicated for each

workstream.

Areas of Cooperation per the Four ISF Strategic Objectives

4,2,1 Promoting Safety, Stability and Security

The BPST will support the DG ISF to promote safety, security, and stability. The
Programme will focus on aligning public order management with international standards

and the strengthening of strategic ccoperation with other security institutions.

e Public Order Management: Assist in the development of a roadmap for
implementation of the ISF Public Order Manual of Guidance, supporting the ISF
alignment with international standards on the use of force, respect of human rights
and protection of public freedoms. Activities will include training and mentoring as
well as the development of operational guidelines and tools. (ISF Strategic Plan:
Project 1.3.1-A).

« ISF/LAF Interoperability: Enable the ISF to fulfil its internal security mandate as
prescribed by law and improve cooperation, coordination and information sharing
between the ISF and the LAF in relation to planning and management of public order
events and response to protests. (ISF Strategic Plan: Program 1.1.2).

4.2.2 Reinforcing Partnerships with the Community

Building partnerships with the community is a cornerstone of moving towards a
community-based approach to policing. The BPST will support the ISF in consolidating
community policing approaches and promoting communication and collaboration
between the police and the community to help prevent and solve problems of crime and
safety.
