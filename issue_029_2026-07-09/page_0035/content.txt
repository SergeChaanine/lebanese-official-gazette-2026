6. The British Embassy's Commitments

The British Embassy commits to support the Programme over a three-year period (2019-
2022) and, subject to [HMG] approvals and available funds, expects to allocate a total
budget of up to GBP 18.5 Million to the Programme. Specifically, the British Embassy will:

Implement the Programme with openness and transparency;

ii. Respond to defined requests and priorities from the MoiM and ISF in a timely,
flexible and efficient manner;

iii. Inform the ISF through the SPMC of any significant Programme changes;

iv. Provide secretarial support for the SPMC, supporting the Head of the ISF Strategic
Planning Team (Head of the ISF Academy); :

v. Establish the British Policing Support Programme in accordance with what is stated
under section 4 and to conduct all of the activities in close collaboration and active
partnership with the beneficiary with a constant focus on building the sustainable
national capabilities of the MoIM and the ISF.

7. Programme timeline

The signatories will work together to ensure that activities are fully implemented over the
period September 2019- 317 of March 2022.

8. MoU-Amendment

The present Memorandum of Understanding may be amended in writing in agreement
between the Participants.

9. Disputes

Any dispute about the interpretation or application of this Memorandum will be resolved by
consultation between the Participants, and will not be referred to any national or
international tribunal or third party for settlement.

10.Entry into effect

This Memorandum will come into operation on signature and will continue in operation
