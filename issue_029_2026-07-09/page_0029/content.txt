1. Introduction

The United Kingdom (UK) has provided assistance to the internal Security Forces of Lebanon
(ISF) since 2008. Significant developments have been achieved in a number of areas resulting
in increased ISF professionalism, improved responsiveness to communities, enhanced
accountability and greater public trust in the ISF. Building on the success of previous
assistance, the UK is committed to continue its support to the Ministry of Interior and
Municipalities and the ISF by launching the British Policing Support Programme ١1 (BPSP II).

A major milestone in the ISF transformation towards a modern police service was the
development of the five-year ISF Strategic Plan 2018-2022. The Plan sets out an ambitious
vision for the ISF and establishes its mission to deliver a pioneering police service that
promotes trust, respects human rights, safeguards freedoms, preserves order and security,
and combats crime efficiently in partnership with the community. The Plan provides a
framework for the ISF’s international partners to provide targeted and coordinated support
in assisting the ISF to achieve its strategic objectives: Promote safety, security and stability;
reinforce partnerships with the community; protect human rights and enhance
accountability; and improve professionalism, organisational efficiency and effectiveness. The
Ministry of Interior and Municipalities (MoIM) and the UK are also extending the partnership
to a more strategic level aimed at strengthening the governance and oversight role played

by the Ministry.

2, Purpose

The purpose of the BPSP ١١ is to support the ISF to improve effectiveness, responsiveness and
accountability to communities, in line with international good practice and human rights
standards, and to enhance the role of the MoIM and build its capacities for effective
strategic governance and donor coordination. The Participants will work in close
collaboration with a focus on building sustainable capabilities aligned with local needs to
support an enhancement of the MoIM's governance role, help create a more trusted ISF and
enhance public safety and better management of Lebanon's internal security challenges.

3. Participants

understanding between the following participants:

+ The Lebanese Ministry of Interior and Municipalities hereafter referred to as “The

MolM"”
* The Directorate General of the Internal Security Forces hereafter referred to as “DG ISF”

* The Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office of the United Kingdom of Great

stipulated in the present Memorandum of Understanding, to implement the British Policing

behalf of the British Embassy in Beirut by Siren Associates as the British Policing Support
Team (BPST). :
