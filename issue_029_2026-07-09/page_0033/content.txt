Maintain the Strategic Programme Management Committee (SPMC) to oversee the
implementation of the ISF Strategic Plan and the Programme. The SPMC will be Co-
chaired by the ISF Director-General and by Her Majesty's Ambassador to Lebanon,
comprising membership from the British Embassy and the British Policing Support
Team, the Heads of PMCs, representatives of relevant Departments and the
Secretariat referred to in section ili. The SPMC will meet quarterly to review
programme delivery and to resolve any strategic issues, obstacles, human rights
concerns or other risks for successful achievement of the Programme objectives;

vil. Inform the British Embassy through the SPMC of any significant request concerning
Programme change.

vi.

Community Centred Approach

Ensure that the approach of the Operations Rooms at HQ level and the Police of
Belrut will focus on the institutionalisation of key community policing practices, i.e.,
community engagement;

ix. To encourage and facilitate the involvement cf civil society organisations and
representatives in developing joint safety and security plans, in alignment with a key
component of the community policing approach.

x. To devolve responsibility to the Heads of Territorial Companies and Police Stations in

Beirut to do community consultations and to implement initiatives to address
community problems and concerns identified in such consultations.

vil.

Human Rights and Accountability
xi The MolM and the DG ISF remain demonstrably committed to international human
rights standards and transparency and accountability, and to strengthening

performance in this area by conducting transparent investigations of alleged human
rights abuses and breaches of the ISF Code of Conduct and holding responsible ISF

members accountable;

xii. To formalise the approval of the ISF Public Order Manual of Guidance so that all ISF
operational departments comply with it when responding to public protests and to
observe the command structure, planning processes, decision-making model and
graduated use of force laid down in the Manual;

xiii. Ensure that international human rights standards are integrated into every aspect of
the Programme's implementation;

xiv. Ensure oversight, public accountability and transparency mechanisms to adhere to
Human Rights standards, and engage constructively with national and international
human rights organisations;

xv. Ensure that any technologies or capabilities developed with the support of the
Programme are not used or operationalised in order to abuse or infringe upon the
human rights of any individual;

xvl. Facilitate adoption of Standard Operating Procedures and internal regulations to
enable the Human Rights Division to work on aligning the ISF processes and
operations with human rights standards and allow it to discharge the mandate
assigned to it by law.
