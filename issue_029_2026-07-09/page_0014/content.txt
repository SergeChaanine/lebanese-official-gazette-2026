Article 9
Trade Secret (Know-How)

Conducting joint activity in the course of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation shall not
create any obligation for the States of the Parties, and/or their natural persons, and/or their legal
entities to provide and/or transfer their background Trade Secrets (Know-How).

The receiving Party, and/or Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) of the
State of such Party shall recognize and protect the Trade Secrets (Know-How), which are
marked as such by the transferring Party, and/or Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors
(Subcontractors) of the State of such Party .

Trade Secrets (Know-How) shall be treated in accordance with the legislation of the state of
either Party .

The receiving Party, and/or Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) of the
State of such Party shall grant to the data which contain Trade Secrets (Know-How), a level of
protection not lower than the level of protection ensured by the transferring Party, and/or
Authorized Entities and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) of the State of such Party .

Transfer and use of Trade Secrets (Know-How) shall be only effected once the measures are
taken by the receiving Party, and/or Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors (Subcontractors)

of the State of such Party for their protection .

Trade Secrets (Know-How) shall not be provided or transferred to a third state, international
organization, as well as to any third Party without the prior written consent of the transferring
Party, and/or Authorized Entities and/or Contractors (Subcontractors) of the State of such

Party.

Information jointly obtained by the Parties, and/or Authorized Entities and/or Contractors
(Subcontractors) shall be recognized as Trade Secret (Know-How) by mutual agreement of the
Parties and/or Authorized Entities.
