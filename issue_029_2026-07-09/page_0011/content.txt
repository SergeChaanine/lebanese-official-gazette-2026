Article 4
Interaction between the Parties

- Interaction between the Parties within the framework of this Agreement shall be
implemented by way of:

- Coordination of issues related to legal protection of the Results of Intellectual Activity and
Intellectual Property Protection;

- Implementation of measures for prevention and suppression of infringements with regard
to Results of Intellectual Activity and Intellectual Property;

- Sharing experience and information on the issues of the legal protection of the Results of
Intellectual Activity and Intellectual Property protection (including within the framework
of international cooperation between the States of the Parties);

- Provision, at the request of either Party, of the laws and regulations of the other Party's
State governing the issues of use and legal protection of the Results of Intellectual Activity
and Intellectual Property protection;

- Coordination of issues regarding transfer and use by the Parties of technologics including
in the interests of any third states, international organizations or in the interests of any
other third Party);

- Implementation of other forms of cooperation agreed upon by the Parties.

Article 5
-Contracts-

In the contracts concluded in the course of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation, the
Authorized Entities shall provide for the clauses concerning the following:

- Distribution of the rights to jointly developed Results of Intellectual Activity and the
revenues from its use with due consideration of the contribution made by the State of each
Party, and /or Authorized Entities, and/or Contractors (subcontractors);
