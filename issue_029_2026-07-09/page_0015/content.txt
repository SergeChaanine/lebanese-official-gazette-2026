Article 11
Settlement of Disputes

If any disputes arise between the contractors (Subcontractors) with regard to the Results of
Intellectual Activity and/or Intellectual Property during joint efforts in the course of bilateral
Military-Technical Cooperation, the Contractors (subcontractors) shall turn to the Authorized
Entity of the state of their respective Party demanding to resolve the arising disputes.

If the-Authorized=Entifies=fail-to=settle-disputes-between the Contractors (Subcontractors) on
their own, they shall engage the working group established in accordance with Article 10 of
this Agreement or the Competent Authorities of the parties into the settlement of the disputes,
or settle the arising disputes and differences in any other way provided for by the contracts

concluded by them.

If any disputes arise between the Authorized Entities, they shall engage the working group
established in accordance with Article 10 of this Agreement, or the Competent Authorities of
the parties into the settlement of the disputes, or settle the arising disputes and differences in
any other way provided for by contracts concluded by them.

Disputes between the parties arising in connection with the implementation and interpretation
of this Agreement shall be settled by way of consultations and ncgotiations between the
Competent Authorities of the parties.
