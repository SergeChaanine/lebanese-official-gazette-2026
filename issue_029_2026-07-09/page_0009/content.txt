الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ‎VA‏ - 75/17/1.؟ ‎voya‏

The Government of the Russian Federation and the. Government of the Lebanese Republic,
hereinafter referred 10 as the Parties,

Taking into account the Agreement between the Government of the Russian Federation and the
Govemment of the Lebanese Republic on Military-Technical Cooperation signed on February
25, 2010.

Reaffirming the intent to fortify friendly relations between the two States,

Wishing to contribute to a better mutual understanding and cooperation in the field of
Intellectual Property in the course of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation.

Expressing their interest in the need for coordination of the Parties’ efforts and undertaking of
effective measures to prevent and suppress any infringements concerning Intellectual Property
in the course of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation,

Adhering to the principles of equality and mutual benefit,
Have agreed as follows :
Article 1
Definitions

The term "Intellectual Property" shall be interpreted herein as described in Article 2 of the
Convention Establishing the World Intellectual Property Organization signed in Stockholm on
July 14, 1967.

The terms used in this Agreement shall be interpreted as follows:

"Defense Products” - weapons, military equipment, works, services and Results of Intellectual
Activity including exclusive rights to such results (Intellectual Property) and information of
Military-Technical domain, excluding information that can be published in mass media,
scientific, literary, and artistic works, and advertising material following the legislation of the
Stateroferther-Party;

"Results of Intellectual Activity" - scientific, engineering, technical, and technological process
solutions contained in the technical and scientific - and - technical documents as well as within
the Defense Products that are developed, produced, and supplied in the course of bilateral
Military-Technical Cooperation:

"Trade Secret (Know-How)" - information of any kind (industrial, technical, economic,
organizational and other) on the Results of Intellectual Activity within the scientific - and -
technical domain and on the means of carrying out professional activities that have actual or
potential commercial value due to its not being known to third parties, provided that third
parties have no legitimate free access to it and that the owner of such information takes
reasonable measures to ensure its confidentiality (including by means of the introduction of the
commercial secret regime);
