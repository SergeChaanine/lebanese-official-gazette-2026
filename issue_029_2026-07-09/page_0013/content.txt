Article 8
Measures for Intellectual Property protection

The parties acknowledge that the Defense Products created, used, or transferred in the course
of bilateral Military-Technical Cooperation may contain and/or constitute Results of
Intellectual Activity and/or Intellectual Property owned by the States of the Parties, and/or their
natural persons, and/or their legal entities .

Each Party shall take necessary measures in accordance with the legislation of the State of such

Party and the international treaties to which the state of such Party is Party to prevent and/or

suppress transfer or provision to any third state, international organizations, as well as any third

Party of the Defense Products containing and/or constituting Results of Intellectual Activity

and/or Intellectual Property owned by the State of the other Party, and/or their natural persons
