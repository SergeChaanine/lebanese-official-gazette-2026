In line with its mandate to assess corruption risks in the public
sector using scientifically grounded methodologies, the
Commission prepared the National Report on Corruption Risks in
the Public Sector, which serves as a key analytical framework for
identifying structural gaps and vulnerabilities in administrative
systems and procedures Prior to its final publication, the
Commission forwarded a copy of the report to the Prime
Minister and the Banque du Liban, as part of efforts to monitor
progress in strengthening the corruption risk management
framework This initiative aligns with relevant national and
international obligations, particularly those aimed at addressing
the deficiencies that led to Lebanon's inclusion on the “List of
Jurisdictions Under Increased Monitoring - Grey List”.
