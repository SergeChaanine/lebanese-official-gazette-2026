During the reporting period, the NACC was invited to provide its
opinion and contribute to discussions on a draft law amending
Law No 175 of 8 May 2020, within the sessions of the Parliamentary
Administration and Justice Committee.

The proposed amendment seeks to broaden the scope of the
law to include the prevention and combating of corruption in
the private sector. This would be achieved by revising the title of
the law, introducing a dedicated section addressing corruption
offenses in the private sector, defining its scope of application,
and establishing the liability of legal persons for acts of corruption
committed for their benefit. It further introduces preventive
obligations related to transparency, compliance, and corruption
prevention within companies and economic and professional
entities

The proposed amendment also introduces significant changes to
the powers and functions of the NACC, including:

Expanding its mandate to receive disclosures of corruption in
the private sector and refer them to the competent judicial

Reinforcing the Commission's independence through

administrative body, budget, and operational safeguards.

The Commission participated in discussions on this draft law
in its capacity as the specialized technical authority, providing
observations and recommendations aimed at:

Ensuring that the legislative text is clear and consistent with
the existing legal framework;
