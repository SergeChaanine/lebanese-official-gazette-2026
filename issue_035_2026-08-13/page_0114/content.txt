* Regional Training on Integrating Digital Technologies into Anti-Corruption
Policies in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan- June 2025: The NACC participated
in a regional training-of-trainers course titled “Integrating Digital Technologies
into Anti-Corruption Policies and Programs”, held in Amman, Jordan, from 1-2
June 2025, represented by Dr. Ali Badran, a member of the Commission. The
training was organized by the Innovation and Training Center of the Integrity
and Anti-Corruption Commission of Jordan, in collaboration with the United
Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and with support from the Korea
International Cooperation Agency (KOICA)

* Regional Workshop on “Harnessing the Power of Data to Promote the Anti-
Corruption and Development Nexus” - Amman, Jordan, May 2025: The workshop
was organized by the UNDP Regional Programme for Arab States within the
framework of its partnership with the Arab Anti-Corruption and Integrity
Network (ACINET), and in cooperation with the Korea International Cooperation
Agency (KOICA) and the Saudi Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority (Nazaha).
