The right of access to information is granted to all natural or
legal persons, provided it is not misused, regardless of their |
status, interest, or purpose in using this information. Requests |
are submitted to the relevant public administration, which may |
issue either an implicit or explicit decision. An implicit decision |
occurs when the administration refrains from providing the |
information An explicit decision may be positive or negative: a |
positive decision resolves the request, while a negative decision

may be appealed
