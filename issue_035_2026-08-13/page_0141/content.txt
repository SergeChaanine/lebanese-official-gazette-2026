Lawyer Fawaz Salem Kabbara
Vice President

Mr. Kabbara joined Tripoli Bar Association in 1977. He started his career in the
Courts of Appeal, and provided legal, financial and investment consultancies
and banking management locally and internationally until 2021. Throughout this
period, Kabbara was also involved in academic and educational work on trade
and business laws, human rights and the principles of governance in a number of
universities in Lebanon.

In addition to his participation and lectures in financial, legal and economic
seminars, Fawaz Kabbara was a member of Lebanese and French arbitration, union
and legal committees and agencies, and a founding member of various financial,
cultural, economic and humanitarian professional associations.

He is the author of many publications and articles on transportation laws,
international trade laws, principles of human rights and governance, in the
economic and political fields, and insocial work He holds certificates of appreciation
in Lebanon and abroad and has the French Recognition Medal.

Between Lebanon, France and the Netherlands, he studied Lebanese law, private
law, international law, and political sciences, and earned a Master's degree in civil
law and a state doctorate in law from the Faculty of Law at the University of Aix-
en-Provence - France

‎Judge Therese Allawi‏ كاذ

‎Member

‎Judge Therese Allawi began her career as a staff member of the Ministry of
Education and Higher Education in 1972, then worked as a judicial assistant from
1974 to 1985, and later became a judge. She assumed since 1992 the position of a
district judge in Mount Lebanon, and during the period between 1990 and 2019,
she presided and served as a member in various chambers and committees in the
courts of first instance and appeal in Beirut and the South, as well as becoming
a member of the Judicial Council in 2014 Judge Allawi held an advisory role in
several chambers at the Court of Cassation and appeal before retiring in 2019 and
getting appointed to the position of honor in 2020.

‎Judge Therese Allawi has a long experience in the educational and academic
fields, as a lecturer at the Faculty of Law at the Lebanese University for nearly
three decades. She also participated in the discussion committees for doctoral
and master’s theses. She has extensive judiciary expertise, including with lawsuits,
courts of appeal and cassation, and the Public Prosecution at the Court of cassation.
