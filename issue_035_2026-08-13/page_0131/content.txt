Protecting and incentivizing whistleblowers in accordance with the provisions |
of the Whistleblower Protection Law |

Article 3 of the Whistleblower Protection Law No. 83 of 10 October |
2018 confers additional powers upon the Commission, beyond |
its investigative and prosecutorial powers under its establishing |
law These powers include:

Safequarding whistleblowers from employment-related and
physical harm;

Granting rewards and providing assistance to whistleblowers,

Initiating proceedings before the competent disciplinary |
or judicial authorities against any individual who causes
employment-related or non-employment related harm to a
whistleblower, including those referred to in Article 12 of this
Law;
