The year 2025 was also marked by significant efforts to strengthen institutional
integration within Lebanon's national anti-corruption framework. The NACC
assumed a leading role in the work of the Ministerial Anti-Corruption Committee
(MACCom), its supporting Technical Committee (TACCom), and the specialized task
teams, while preparing to assume the chairmanship of the task team responsible
for aligning national legislation with international standards. In addition, the NACC
began exploring the use of advanced technologies, including artificial intelligence,
for the secure collection, analysis, and storage of information, thereby enhancing
its oversight and analytical capacities.

Chapter Two of the report focuses on the NACC's fulfilment of its duties as defined
by the relevant laws, which include:

* Receiving, registering, processing, and reviewing assets and interests
declarations in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Assets and
Interests Declarations and the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment

* Promoting a culture of integrity within public administrations and institutions
as well as society at large, while providing the necessary knowledge and tools
to prevent and combat corruption.

* Receiving corruption disclosures, investigating and examining corruption
offenses, and, where appropriate, referring them to the competent regulatory,
disciplinary, or judicial authorities.

* Monitoring the state, costs and causes of corruption, as well as efforts to
prevent and combat it, in accordance with applicable laws, adopted policies,
and binding bilateral and multilateral agreements, and preparing periodic or
special reports for publication in the Official Gazette and on the NACC website

* Providing opinions, either proactively or at the request of the competent
authorities, on legislation, decrees, decisions, draft legal instruments, policies,
and strategies related to preventing and combating corruption

* Protecting and incentivizing whistleblowers in accordance with the provisions
of the Whistleblower Protection Law

* Receiving and examining complaints related to the implementation of the Right
of Access to Information Law and issuing decisions thereon; providing guidance
to the competent authorities on its implementation; preparing and publishing
an annual report on the matter; and contributing to public awareness efforts
aimed at promoting and strengthening this right.

* Promoting transparency in the petroleum sector by ensuring the proper
implementation of the Law on Enhancing Transparency inthe Petroleum Sector;
receiving and examining complaints related to its implementation and issuing
decisions thereon; and providing guidance to the competent authorities.

* Contributing to the implementation of the Law on the Recovery of Assets
Derived from Corruption Crimes through the dedicated department within
the NACC tasked with planning, monitoring, and coordinating asset recovery
efforts
