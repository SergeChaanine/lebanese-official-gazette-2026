٠ Participating in the adoption of conference resolutions: Draft resolutions and

* Second Global Conference on Harnessing Data to Improve Corruption
Measurement, December 2025: Judge Claude Karam, President of the Com-
mission, participated in the Second Global Conference on Harnessing Data to
Improve Corruption Measurement, held in New York City from 2 to 4 December
2025 The conference was organized in cooperation with the United Nations Office
on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) and the International Anti-Corruption Academy
(IACA). Judge Karam represented the Lebanese Republic in the main sessions of
the conference, where he presented the Commission's vision on the conference
theme and highlighted its efforts to strengthen international cooperation and
develop joint initiatives, emphasizing the importance of continued collaboration
with partners to exchange expertise and build capacities.

On the margins of the conference, the President held a series of productive
bilateral meetings with senior international officials and representatives of
various authorities and organizations, including:

٠ Mr. Marcos Athias Neto, United Nations Assistant Secretary-General
٠ HE Humaid Abu Shabas, President of the UAE Supreme Audit Institution

٠ Mr Drago Kos, current Dean of the International Anti-Corruption Academy
(IACA)

These meetings focused on enhancing joint cooperation, exploring future
initiatives, and identifying opportunities to develop programs of mutual interest.
In this context, the President of the Commission reaffirmed the Commission's
commitment to strengthening its international partnerships in support of its
strategic objectives.

* Official Meeting with the Governor of the National Transparency Authority
(NTA) of Greece - November 2025: Judge Claude Karam, President of the NACC,
held an official meeting with Ms. Alexandra Rogakou, Governor of the National
Transparency Authority (NTA) in Greece, during his visit to Athens on 10
November 2025. The meeting was part of the Commission's ongoing efforts to
strengthen international cooperation in the fight against corruption.

* Third Arab Forum on Enhancing Transparency and Combating Corruption in
Cairo- October 2025: Judge Claude Karam, President of the NACC, participated t
in the Third Arab Forum on Enhancing Transparency and Combating Corruption
in the Digital Age, held in Cairo, Egypt, on 22 -23 October 2025. The event brought
together a distinguished group of heads of commissions, officials, experts, and
specialists from various countries.
