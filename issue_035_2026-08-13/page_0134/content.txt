The complainant may file an appeal against the relevant
administration before the Commission, which is the sole
authority competent to review such matters following its
establishment The Commission has the authority to require the
public administration to provide the documents and information
requested by the complainant, provided it determines that
such information does not fall within the exceptions set forth in
Articles 4 and 5 of the Right of Access to Information Law These
exceptions stipulate that the information must not be confidential
nor classified as personal data.

administration has been notified in writing of the complainant's

once—has elapsed.

As part of its efforts to monitor the proper implementation of
the law, the Commission prepared its first annual report for 2023,
which was released on April 3, 2025, based on a survey conducted
with government departments at both the national and local
levels.

The survey resulted in 190 successful interviews out of 310
departments at the national level and 787 successful interviews
out of 1123 local departments. This allowed for a coverage rate
of 61% at the level of government departments and 82% at the
level of municipalities and federations of municipalities.
