Appendix No. 2:The Budget of the National Anti-Corruption
Commission and closing balance of account for the year 2025
“Unverified”

First: Budget for the year 2025

1- The Commission's revenues and expenditures for the 2025 budget proposal are
estimated at LBP 66,444,000,000, to be financed through a contribution allocated
in the State Budget for 2025, as approved under the 2025 Budget Law and pursuant
to Ministry of Finance Decision No. 136/10 dated 6 February 2025.

The Commission was also granted a treasury advance pursuant to Decree No.
14319 dated 29 November 2024, in the amount of LBP 15,000,000,000. This
advance is to be repaid through an appropriation to be allocated for this purpose
in the Commission's 2026 budget. The advance was intended to cover the monthly
remuneration of the President and members of the Commission, together with all
related allowances and entitlements, for the years 2024 and 2025, following the
amendment of Article 1 of the aforementioned Decree on 11 April 2025 to include
the year 2025.
