As stipulated in Article 1 of Chapter | of Personnel, Structure, and Terms of
Appointment of the NACC, the organizational structure of the Commission consists
of executive units, administrative units, support units, and an internal control unit
The executive units comprise five directorates, each headed by a director and
supported by staff members reporting to them, namely

1. The Directorate of Corruption Disclosures and Protection of Whistleblowers
2. The Directorate of the Right of Access to Information

3. The Directorate of Assets and Interests Declarations, and the Punishment of
Illicit Enrichment

4. The Directorate of Awareness and Promotion of the Culture of Integrity and
Research

5. The Directorate of Investigation and Enquiry

The administrative units and supporting services consist of two directorates. the
Directorate of Administrative Affairs and Supporting Services and the Directorate
of Legal Affairs. The former includes the following departments:

1. Department of Human Resources

2. Department of Administrative Affairs and Services
3. Department of Accounting and Financial Affairs
4. Department of Information
