The following Workshops were organized:

* Legal Seminar for Trainee and Practicing Lawyers - 9 October 2025: The NACC,
in cooperation with the Beirut Bar Association, organized a legal seminar titled
“The Implementation of the New Law on Asset and Interest Declarations and
the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment.” The event took place in the Grand Lecture
Hall at the Beirut Bar Association's headquarters on Thursday, October 9, 2025,
and attended by trainee and practicing lawyers. This initiative comes as part of
ongoing coordination between the NACC and the Beirut Bar Association, and
within the framework of the cooperation between the NACC and UNDP under
the project "Anti-Corruption for Trust in Lebanon” (ACT), implemented with the
support of the European Union and the Government of the Kingdom of Denmark

* Workshop for Public Prosecutors and Investigative Judges- 7 October 2025: The
NACC organized a workshop for public prosecutors and investigative judges
on the implementation of the new Law on Asset and Interest Declarations and
the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment, on October 7, 2025, at the Sofitel Beirut Le
Gabriel Hotel in Achrafieh The workshop comes as part of ongoing coordination
between the NACC, the High Judicial Council, and the Office of the Public
Prosecutor atthe Court of Cassation, and withinthe framework of the cooperation
between the Commission and UNDP through the “Anti-Corruption for Trust in
Lebanon” (ACT) project, implemented with the support of the European Union
and the Government of the Kingdom of Denmark. The workshop aimed to shed
light on the new illicit enrichment law by outlining key elements related to the
practical application of its provisions.

partnership with UNDP organized a specialized training aimed at strengthening
