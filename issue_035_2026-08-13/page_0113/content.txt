* RegionalMeetingon “Combating Corruptionand Promoting Integrity -[ 062025:
The NACC, represented by Dr. Kleib Kleib, participated in a high-level regional
meeting titled “Combating Corruption and Promoting Integrity in Arab Countries:
Progress Achieved and Priorities for the Next Phase” The meeting was held in
Amman, Jordan, on 29 June, with broad participation from key stakeholders,
education leaders, policymakers, and oversight bodies from across the region.
The meeting provided an important platform to review progress in combating
corruption and promoting integrity principles in Arab countries, discuss current
challenges, and develop a roadmap for the next phase to strengthen good
governance and institutional transparency across sectors

* Regional Workshop on “Promoting Integrity and Combating Corruption: The
Role of the Education Sector- June 2025: The NACC, represented by Dr Kleib
Kleib, participated in a regional workshop on integrity in the education sector,
held in Amman, Jordan, on June 26-27, 2025. The workshop was organized by
the regional project “Combating Corruption and Promoting Integrity” under the
United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in collaboration with the Arab
Anti-Corruption and Integrity Network (ACINET), with support from the Korea
International Cooperation Agency (KOICA)
