Article 14 of the Whistleblower Protection Law empowers the
Commission to grant a reward and/or provide assistance to
a whistleblower in either of the following situations: (1) if the
disclosure results in the administration obtaining funds or other
benefits, including the collection of fines or the recovery of assets;
or (2) if the disclosure prevents the administration from incurring
a loss or material damage Furthermore, the Commission
may, upon request, offer suitable legal or financial support to
whistleblowers who have suffered material or physical harm as
a result of their disclosure.
