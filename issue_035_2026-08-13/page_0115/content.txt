World Bank Global Forum 2025 in Washington, D.C. - April 2025: The NACC of
Lebanon, represented by its President Judge Claude Karam and Member Dr.
Joe Maalouf, participated in the World Bank Global Forum 2025, held on April
8-9 at the World Bank's headquarters in Washington, D.C, under the theme
“Partnerships for Anti-Corruption”. On the margins of the forum, the delegation
held several meetings with participating delegations to exchange views and
experiences, and agreed to maintain ongoing communication The delegation
also visited the International Monetary Fund (INF) headquarters and held an
extensive meeting with several officials. During the meeting, the delegation
presented the Commission's mandate, achievements, challenges, and its key
priorities for the foreseeable future President Karam subsequently continued
meetings with officials from the US. Department of State and Department of
the Treasury.
