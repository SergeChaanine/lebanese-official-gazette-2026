Chapter Two: Performing the Functions Mandated to the NACC by Law

Overview of the Commission's Functions and Achievements During 2025

Function 1: Receiving, registering, processing, and reviewing asset
and interest declarations in accordance with the provisions of the
Law on Asset and Interest Declarations and the Punishment of Illicit
Enrichment.

Function 2: Promoting a culture of integrity within public
administrations and institutions as well as society at large, while
providing the necessary knowledge and tools to prevent and combat
corruption.

Function 3: Receiving corruption disclosures, examining and
investigating corruption offences, and referring them, when necessary,
to the competent oversight, disciplinary and judicial authorities
