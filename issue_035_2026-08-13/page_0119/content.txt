Overview of the Commission's Functions and Achievements During 2025

Receiving, registering, processing, and reviewing asset and interest declarations
in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Asset and Interest Declarations
| and the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment.

Article 5 of the Law on Assets and Interests Declarations and
the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment (Law No. 189 of 16 October
2020) provides that the declarations prescribed therein shall
be submitted to the Commission, with a receipt issued to the
declarant, and maintained in both physical and electronic
registers. Article 6 of the same law further provides that the
Commission, as well as any entity temporarily entrusted with
receiving such declarations, shall issue a circular, through
| the department to which civil servants subject to declaration
| Legal Powers: | requirements are affiliated, within one month of the law's
publication. It also requires the issuance, during the first month
of each year, of a circular reminding concerned parties of their
obligation to comply with the provisions of the law, including the
applicable deadlines for submitting declarations and the legal
consequences of non-compliance.

The Commission is further vested with the authority to examine
the content of declarations, thereby enabling it to exercise its
powers proactively to investigate offences that may arise from
its audit activities, in particular false declarations and illicit
enrichment.
