- Conducting studies and research, and publishing reports,

٠ Raising public awareness of the causes and consequences of

corruption, as well as the means to combat and prevent it, using |

all available channels. This includes developing programs to
educate and foster a culture of integrity, particularly through

educational institutions, universities, media outlets, and civil

society organizations

٠ Encouraging the Ministry of Education and Higher Education,
as well as other public and private educational institutions, and
collaborating with them to integrate theoretical and practical
content on integrity and anti-corruption into their curricula,

and support the development of these materials and related |

educational tools.

٠ Organizing conferences, seminars, and training programs for
various public and private bodies on all subjects within the
Commission's mandate.

Drawing on its mandate to advance education, raise awareness,
and develop specialized knowledge to promote integrity and
prevent and combat corruption, and building on initiatives
launched in 2023 and 2024, the Directorate of Awareness and
Promotion of the Culture of Integrity and Research at the NACC
continued to implement its programs and activities throughout
2025, despite the significant challenges posed by the prolonged
conflict in Lebanon.

During the year, the Commission's efforts were guided by
integrated priorities aligned with its legal mandate. These
included organizing workshops and discussion forums,
participating in cultural and specialized events, delivering
targeted training programs, and preparing and signing
Memoranda of Understanding with educational and academic
institutions.
