Law No. 175/2020 on Combating Corruption in the Public Sector and the
Establishment of the National Anti-Corruption Commission, together with other
relevant legislation, entrusts the Commission with a comprehensive mandate
encompassing the following tasks:

1. Managing the asset and interest declaration system, including the receipt,
registration, administration, and review of such declarations.

2. Receiving corruption disclosures and related complaints, examining and
investigating corruption offences, either proactively orin response to submitted
disclosures, and referring them, when necessary, to the competent oversight,
disciplinary and judicial authorities.

3. Protecting whistleblowers, ensuring the legal safequards afforded to them
and providing incentives where appropriate.

4. Coordinating efforts to recover assets derived from corruption offences, and
managing the special fund established for this purpose.

5. Ensuring the proper implementation of the Right of Access to Information
Law.

6. Ensuring the proper implementation of the Law on Enhancing Transparency
in the Petroleum Sector.

7. Monitoring corruption trends, including its costs, causes, and efforts to combat
and prevent it, in light of applicable laws, adopted policies, and relevant
international conventions, and preparing and publishing special or periodic
reports on these matters.

8. Providing opinions, either proactively or upon request by the competent
authorities, on legislation, decrees, decisions and draft instruments, as well
as on policies and strategies related to combating and preventing corruption.

9. Promoting a culture of integrity across public administrations, public
institutions, and society, while strengthening the knowledge and awareness
required to combat and prevent corruption.

10. Participating, through the President of the Commission, in the formation of
the committee responsible for evaluating nominations for membership of the
Public Procurement Authority.
