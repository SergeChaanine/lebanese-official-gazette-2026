Function 4: Monitoring the state, costs and causes of corruption, as
well as efforts to prevent and combat it, in accordance with applicable
laws, adopted policies, and binding bilateral and multilateral
agreements, and preparing periodic or special reports for publication
in the Official Gazette and on the NACC website

Function 5: Providing opinions, either proactively or at the request
of the competent authorities, on legislation, decrees, decisions, draft
legal instruments, policies, and strategies related to preventing and
combating corruption

Function 6: Protecting and incentivizing whistleblowers in accordance
with the provisions of the Whistleblower Protection Law

Function 7: Receiving and examining complaints related to the
implementation of the Right of Access to Information Law and issuing
decisions thereon; providing guidance to the competent authorities
on its implementation; preparing and publishing an annual report

on the matter; and contributing to public awareness efforts aimed at
promoting and strengthening this right.

Function 8: Promoting transparency in the petroleum sector by
ensuring the proper implementation of the Law on Enhancing
Transparency in the Petroleum Sector; receiving and examining
complaints related to its implementation and issuing decisions
thereon; and providing guidance to the competent authorities.

Function 9: Contributing to the implementation of the Law on
the Recovery of Assets Derived from Corruption Crimes through
the dedicated department within the NACC tasked with planning,
monitoring, and coordinating asset recovery efforts.
