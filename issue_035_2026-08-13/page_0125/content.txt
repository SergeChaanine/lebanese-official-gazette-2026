4- Cooperation with Educational and Academic Institutions

Pursuant to its legal mandate to encourage and collaborate
with the Ministry of Education and Higher Education, as well as
other educational institutions, in embedding integrity and anti-
corruption principles within academic curricula, the Commission
singed Memoranda of Understanding in 2025 with the American
University of Beirut (AUB), the Lebanese American University
(LAU), and the Center for Educational Research and Development.

The Memoranda aim to:

Develop comprehensive theoretical and practical educational
resources on integrity and anti-corruption;

Incorporate specialized courses or modules into university
curricula;

Advance academic research and foster scientific collaboration
in this field
