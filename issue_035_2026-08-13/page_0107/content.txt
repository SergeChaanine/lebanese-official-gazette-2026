b. Internal Regulations

Following the adoption of its Internal Regulations and Code of Conduct, the NACC
undertook a review of these instruments to address any potential gaps and further
refine them in light of developments related to the use of modern technological
tools.

These regulations are available on the NACC website www.nacc gov.lb.

c. Human Resources and their Development

No staff members have yet been appointed to the Commission due to delays in
adopting the Internal Regulations and establishing the organizational structure of
its full-time administrative staff, which is to be headed by a Secretary-General These
positions are to be filled by the Commission following a competitive recruitment
process conducted by the Civil Service Board (CSB). Following the Commission's
request, the CSB approved holding a competition to fill 18 administrative positions,
pending the approval of the Council of Ministers. The Commission followed up
on the matter with the Council of Ministers, which granted its approval through
Decision No. 20, issued at its session on 20 November 2025 Subsequently, the
Commission proceeded to coordinate with the CSB to fill the vacant positions.

Within the framework of its cooperation with UNDP, as part of the technical
support provided to activate the Commission's work and strengthen its capacity to
carry out its mandate, UNDP has made available specialized legal experts. It has
also facilitated the engagement of a number of volunteers from local universities
to support the Commission in receiving assets and interests declarations from
civil servants, in addition to providing administrative assistance to its various
directorates The NACC has further worked, with the technical support provided
by UNDP advisors, to develop the rules and procedures governing the Youth
Volunteering Program implemented by the Commission in cooperation with donor
partners, with the aim of further strengthening its capacity to perform its functions
during the current phase
