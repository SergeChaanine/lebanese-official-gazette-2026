* Workshop on “Detecting Corruption: How to Overcome Challenges” -Kuwait
City, April 2025: The NACC participated in a workshop titled "Detecting
Corruption: How to Overcome Challenges,” held in Kuwait City from 7-10 April

c. Memoranda of Understanding

During 2025, the Commission signed a number of Memoranda of Understanding
(MoUs) with several institutions in the education and higher education sectors, in
line with its mandate and functions. Among the memoranda concluded during the
reporting period are the following:

* Memorandum of Understanding Between the
NACC and the Center for Educational Research
and Development, July 2,2025: The NACC and the
CenterforEducationalResearchand Development
signed a Memorandum of Understanding
at the Commission's headquarters on July 2,
2025, to cooperate in multiple areas of mutual
interest in accordance with applicable laws and
regulations. The memorandum aims to establish
a partnership between the two parties to promote
a culture of integrity and to enhance knowledge
related to the prevention and combating of
corruption This will be pursued by strengthening
: : academic collaboration, training and developing
specialized human capacities, improving curricula, programs, and educational
materials, encouraging relevant research, supporting extracurricular activities,
and organizing competitions
