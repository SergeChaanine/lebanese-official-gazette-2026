Structure and Enhancing its Financial, Administrative,

1. Rehabilitating and Equipping the NACC Headquarters

Throughout 2025, the NACC work continued the rehabilitation and equipping
of its headquarters and the offices of its directorates. The first floor occupied by
the Commission was fully equipped, including investigation rooms designed to
meet international standards, thereby supporting the operations of different
directorates, with special support from UNDP. Previously, with the support of
international partners, particularly UNDP, the United Nations Office on Drugs
and Crime (UNODC), and Expertise France, the Commission had completed the
furnishing and equipping of the 4th through 8th floors. Five floors of the building
are now allocated to the following directorates: the Directorate of Corruption
Disclosures and Protection of Whistleblowers, the Directorate of the Right of
Access to Information, the Directorate of Assets and Interests Declarations, and
the Punishment of Illicit Enrichment, the Directorate of Awareness and Promotion
of the Culture of Integrity and Research, and the Directorate of Investigation and
Enquiry.

Alongside the building outfitting, UNDP continued to provide the Commission with
office supplies necessary for the smooth functioning of its directorates and the full
execution of its mandate under anti-corruption laws. Throughout 2025, essential
stationery was supplied to ensure uninterrupted operations, while maintenance
was carried out on critical energy infrastructure, including solar panels, as well as
on IT systems and servers
