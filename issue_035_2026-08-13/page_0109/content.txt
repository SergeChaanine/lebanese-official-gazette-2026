3. Securing the funding necessary for the Commission to carry out its
mandate

a. On 6 February 2025, pursuant to Decision No 136/1 of the Ministry of Finance,
the NACC was granted a financial contribution for the year 2025 totalling LBP
66,444,000,000 (sixty-six billion four hundred and forty-four million Lebanese
pounds), allocated as follows:

* LBP 36,444,000,000 (contribution for salaries and wages), to cover the
Commission's salary and wage expenditures for 2025;

* LBP 30,000,000,000 (contribution for other current expenditures), to cover the
Commission’s other operational expenses for 2025

b. On 17 January 2025, a treasury advance in the amount of LBP 15,000,000,000
(fifteen billion Lebanese pounds) was credited to the account of the NACC at Banque
du Liban, pursuant to Decree No. 14319 dated 29 November 2024. This advance was
allocated to cover the monthly compensation of the President and members of
the Commission, along with all related allowances and entitlements for the year
2024. The advance shall be reimbursed through an appropriation included for this
purpose in the NACC's 2026 budget.

c. On 11 April 2025, pursuant to Council of Ministers Decision No. 8 dated 11 April
2025, Article 1 of the above-mentioned decree was amended to provide that the
purpose of the advance is to “cover the monthly compensation of the President
and members of the Commission, together with all related allowances and
entitlements, for the years 2024 and 2025, instead of 2024 only.

4. Strengthening Technical and Scientific Cooperation with Relevant
Local and International Entities

a. Specialized Workshops

The NACC, in cooperation with its local partners, international donors, and
international organizations, initiated a series of events aimed at launching
the implementation of the action plan it had previously developed. The plan is
structured around ten key pillars and areas of support, along with operational steps
required to build technical capacities and provide the Commission with resources
needed carry out its mandate effectively

The pillars are as follows: 1) Joint or cross-cutting mandates 2) Investigating corru-
ption offences 3) Monitoring and evaluation 4) Providing advisory opinions, either
proactively or upon request 5) Education and awareness-raising 6) Implementing
the asset and interest declaration system 7) Contributing to the implementation of
the Whistleblower Protection Law 8) Contributing to the implementation of the
Right of Access to Information Law 9) Implementing the Law on the Recovery of
Assets Derived from Corruption Crimes 10) Monitoring the proper implementation
of the Law on Enhancing Transparency in the Petroleum Sector
